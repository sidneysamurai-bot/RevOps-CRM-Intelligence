Week 03 Insight

The original data-quality question was:

How do we identify and remediate CRM data-quality problems?

The Reality Lab expanded the question:

How does CRM data quality affect automation, AI agents, revenue workflows and management decisions?

The operating chain is:

CRM Data Quality
       ↓
Trusted Customer & Opportunity Context
       ↓
Reliable Reporting & Forecasting
       ↓
Automation
       ↓
AI Agents
       ↓
Operational Decisions

Key principle

Data quality is upstream of automation and AI.

A CRM may contain a large volume of records while still being operationally unreliable when records are duplicated, incomplete, inconsistent, incorrectly owned or poorly governed.
